The Challenge

The Croatian Rowing Federation realised that their existing website no longer met modern standards. Built with Adobe Dreamweaver, the website had become difficult to maintain, limiting both usability for visitors and flexibility for updates.

As their needs grew, it became clear that the site required more than a visual refresh. They needed a modern solution that would simplify content management, provide smoother experience across devices.

The challenge was to translate static designs into a fully functional, responsive website, while ensuring that administrators could easily manage and update content without technical expertise.
